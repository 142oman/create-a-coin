# View model validation

Generated by `python -m ladder validate`.

## 1. Calibration on unseen campaigns

Model fitted on the 23 campaigns settled before 2025-01-22; tested on 821 clean posts from campaigns starting on or after it. Each rung is placed where the model says a given share of posts will reach it; the table shows what share actually did.

| Rung | Model says reached by | Actually reached by |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 49.9% | 49.8% |
| 2 | 24.9% | 24.7% |
| 3 | 12.4% | 12.7% |
| 4 | 6.3% | 7.4% |
| 5 | 3.1% | 4.9% |
| 6 | 1.6% | 2.9% |

Close agreement means thresholds really have the odds we claim. The top rungs sit in the tail, where viral posts (not part of the lognormal) make reality slightly heavier than the model.

## 2. Does the model recover what we hid in the data?

The generator multiplied views by a hidden factor per category and per format. The model was never told these. Below: the factor estimated from posts alone vs the hidden truth.

| Factor | Level | Estimated | Hidden truth | Relative to |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| category | D2C | 1.00x | 0.85x | FMCG |
| category | FMCG | 1.00x | 1.00x | FMCG |
| category | entertainment | 1.39x | 1.35x | FMCG |
| category | finance | 0.69x | 0.60x | FMCG |
| category | gaming | 1.19x | 1.20x | FMCG |
| format | carousel | 0.40x | 0.40x | reel |
| format | long_form | 0.26x | 0.30x | short |
| format | reel | 1.00x | 1.00x | reel |
| format | short | 1.00x | 1.00x | short |
