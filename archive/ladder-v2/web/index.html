You set the budget. The market sets the price of a view.

No price cap, no guaranteed minimum. Posts that pass a minimum threshold mint coins, either at each rung they reach or for every view. The budget backs all the coins, and one price per coin is set when the campaign ends. If too few views arrive, you pay the negotiated price and get the rest back.
