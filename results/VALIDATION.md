# Validation

World from recipe seed 2. All computed by `python -m ladder validate`.

## Is the generated world realistic?

- **Heavy tails:** the top 10% of posts carry 88% of views; the 99th-percentile post has 433× the median's views.
- **Growth shapes:** 56% of posts peak on day one, 18% after day 3; 23% have stopped by the campaign's end.
- **Bigger accounts get more views:** correlation of log followers and log views = 0.84.
