# Backtest

Generated by `python -m ladder backtest` from the world made by recipe seed 2 (120 campaigns, 500 creators, 4251 posts). Every past campaign is paid twice on the same posts: the old way (its own gut-feel ladder, the same for everyone, no fraud check, no budget stop) and by Creator Coin (rungs, reference and fraud cut-offs built only from campaigns that had ended before it started). Creator behaviour is held fixed.

## Eight measures

| | Old way | Creator Coin |
|---|---:|---:|
| Campaigns over budget | 22 of 120 | 0 |
| Worst overspend | 6.1× budget | 0.77× (never over) |
| Cost per 1,000 genuine views (median) | ₹58.3 | ₹40.6 |
| Money returned / left unspent | ₹3,810,200 left unspent | ₹3,330,412 thin-market refunds + ₹1,257,803 between rungs |
| Paid for bought views | ₹414,100 | ₹89,643 (₹348,460 caught and never paid) |
| Creators paid, nano | 19% | 79% |
| Creators paid, micro | 42% | 79% |
| Creators paid, mid | 65% | 74% |
| Creators paid, macro | 81% | 74% |
| Fair Reach rescues | – | 17 campaigns, 46 creators paid only because of it |
| Price swing between similar campaigns (CV, lower is steadier) | 0.67 | 0.44 |
| Pay to the top 10% of creators | 50% | 75% |

Pay concentration is higher with Creator Coin because pay follows reach, and reach is concentrated. The old way's flat payouts capped what the biggest posts earned.
